# Next Groups Draft — 로저 해적단 / 록스 해적단

**Research state:** Evidence review / score proposal only  
**App data:** not yet added  
**Model:** Balanced 1.2 · 7 Final Core Stats · Haki Weight 0.5

## 1. Why these two groups

The canonical Group registry already contains `roger-pirates` and `rocks-pirates`.
They are the most useful next expansion because they directly test the current world-top calibration around Prime 몽키 D. 가프.

The target set is seven Characters:
- 로저 해적단: 골 D. 로저 / 실버즈 레일리 / 스코퍼 가반
- 록스 해적단-related calibration: 록스 D. 지벡 / 에드워드 뉴게이트 / 카이도 / 샬롯 링링

Historical membership and Evaluation state are deliberately separated. For example, 카이도 and 샬롯 링링 were Rocks Pirates members in the past, but their proposed Evaluations below use their much better documented Wano-era combat states. Historical Rocks-era membership must not be used as permission to synthesize their younger and later peaks.

## 3. 골 D. 로저

### Canon evidence
- Ch. 966: Kamusari sends Oden away; Roger and Whitebeard then clash with advanced Supreme King Haki and their crews continue fighting for three days and three nights.
- Ch. 1163: Roger joins Garp, Rocks, Newgate, Kaido and Linlin in attacking Imu.
- Ch. 1165: Roger and Garp fight demonized Rocks. Roger explicitly reasons about the interaction of Armament / Supreme King Haki and concludes that they need a critical mass of Supreme King Haki; the combined final attack defeats Rocks.
- ONE PIECE.com: the only pirate called Pirate King, having obtained wealth, fame and power.

### Interpretation
Attack / Technique / Stamina are world-top direct evidence axes. Combat IQ also receives unusually strong direct support from Ch. 1165's in-fight analysis rather than status alone.
Versatility is intentionally the lowest axis because the directly demonstrated combat system is swordsmanship + Haki, even though its quality is extreme.

### Upper-bound limiter
No Devil Fruit or wide toolkit should be invented from status. Pirate King is not an automatic 100 in every stat.

## 4. 실버즈 레일리

### Canon evidence
- ONE PIECE.com: former Roger Pirates vice-captain, Pirate King's right hand, Dark King; overwhelming power remains even in old age.
- Ch. 512: old Rayleigh stops Borsalino's light movement, sword-fights him and cuts his cheek.
- Ch. 597 and associated training material: demonstrates and teaches the three Haki systems and advanced applications.
- Ch. 1059: his reputation helps end the Amazon Lily confrontation, but Rayleigh himself says that at his current age he could not beat Marshall D. Teach head-on.
- Ch. 1161: prime-era Rayleigh directly intervenes against Sommers at God Valley and rescues Shakuyaku.

### Interpretation
The proposed default is Prime E2 because the project is now calibrating the Roger-era legendary band. Current Rayleigh should later become a second Evaluation rather than being mixed into the Prime score.
Old-age direct feats are used as a lower-bound and capability continuity check, not as Prime output data.

### Upper-bound limiter
Prime long-duration combat and full 7-axis direct samples remain sparse. The right-hand title cannot make every stat 95+ automatically.

## 5. 스코퍼 가반

### Canon evidence
- ONE PIECE.com: former Roger Pirates member who fights with two axes.
- Official anime episode 1170 description calls him the Pirate King's left arm who stood alongside Roger and Rayleigh.
- Ch. 1140: current Gaban repeatedly evades Luffy, uses Haki on the key to damage him, then unleashes an axe barrage that forces Zoro to intervene and damages the castle. He yields when Luffy enters Gear 5 and Zoro joins rather than being defeated.
- Ch. 1148-1149: instantly cuts Sommers' arm, prevents regeneration, and then overwhelms/cuts him. His later surrender occurs after Gunko threatens Colon; Ch. 1149 depicts Gaban foreseeing that he cannot save his son in time.
- Ch. 1152: correctly identifies Zoro's Supreme King Haki and explains the need for conscious control.

### Interpretation
Current Gaban has excellent direct Speed / Technique / Combat IQ evidence. Defense and Stamina are deliberately lower because the Gunko sequence is not a clean defensive failure or full endurance test.
No Prime score is created yet: status/reputation is not enough to invent a numeric youth boost.

### Upper-bound limiter
The Luffy/Zoro scene is a test, not a completed serious 1v2. The Sommers anti-regeneration interaction should remain a Special/Matchup mechanism, not be converted into generic Attack superiority.

## 6. 록스 D. 지벡

### State rule
The proposed Evaluation is **natural God Valley-era Rocks before Domi Reversi**.

Demonized Rocks in Ch. 1164-1165 is an externally altered state created by Imu. Its extraordinary regeneration/defense must not be silently assigned to normal Rocks.

### Canon evidence
- ONE PIECE.com: captain of a crew once called the world's strongest; Sengoku describes him as perhaps Roger's first and strongest enemy.
- Ch. 1155: exceptional swordsman; clashes with Harald with enormous Haki output; Supreme King Haki is confirmed; history includes fatally wounding a Marine Admiral and destroying the Gates of Justice.
- Ch. 1162: defeats Figarland Garling while protecting/rescuing his family and rejoins Newgate/Kaido.
- Ch. 1163: natural Rocks attacks Imu with the other top combatants before Imu applies Domi Reversi.
- Ch. 1164-1165: demonized-state feats are recorded as state-specific context only.

### Interpretation
Natural Rocks has enough direct modern flashback evidence to belong in the same legendary band as Roger/Garp/Newgate, but Defense/Stamina remain more uncertain than his Attack/Speed/Technique.

## 7. 에드워드 뉴게이트

### Canon evidence
- ONE PIECE.com: world strongest man; overwhelming combat power; Gura Gura no Mi.
- Official ONE PIECE.com feature: the legendary man who uniquely fought Gol D. Roger evenly.
- Ch. 966: Prime Newgate and Roger clash with Supreme King Haki; their crews battle for three days and nights.
- Ch. 1161/1163: younger Newgate is active at God Valley and joins the attack on Imu.
- Marineford old/ill Newgate remains a lower-bound Stamina/Attack reference only and must not be blended into Prime limitations.

### Interpretation
Roger and Prime Newgate receive the same proposed Overall intentionally, with different internal profiles: Newgate gains more Versatility from quake-based close/ranged/area control while Roger has higher Speed/Combat IQ.

## 8. 카이도

### Canon evidence
- ONE PIECE.com: Beasts Pirates governor and mythical Seiryu user.
- Ch. 951: clashes with Big Mom strongly enough to split the sky.

## 9. 샬롯 링링

### Canon evidence
- ONE PIECE.com: Emperor, Soul-Soul Fruit user.
- Ch. 951: sky-splitting clash with Kaido.
- Ch. 1009: participates with Kaido in Hakai; Law states that their Haki is too strong for direct teleportation.
- Ch. 1011: directly uses Supreme King Haki infusion against Page One.

## 10. Cross-scale conflict check

If these scores are adopted, the legendary band is coherent with Prime Garp 97.429.
However, it exposes a likely compression issue in the existing current top-tier anchors:
- 샹크스 92.571
- 쥬라큘 미호크 92.714
- 쿠잔 92.714
- 사카즈키 92.429
- 보르살리노 92.143
- current 가프 93.714

The new Characters should **not** be lowered merely to fit these old anchors. Before actual implementation, Shanks/Mihawk and possibly the Admiral band should receive a separate cross-calibration pass.

## 11. Multi-membership architecture warning

Whitebeard / Kaido / Big Mom create the first major real case where one Character should have:
- a primary/current or representative crew membership, and
- a historical Rocks Pirates membership.

Current `getCharacterList()` returns one row per Membership and `getStatRanking()` consumes that list directly. Adding multiple Membership rows without refactoring would duplicate the same Character in rankings.

Before implementing these groups:
1. Separate **unique evaluated roster** from **group navigation memberships**.
2. Rankings must deduplicate by Character + default Evaluation.
3. Group pages may intentionally show one Character in multiple historical/current groups.
4. Existing validation that expects exactly one Membership per Character must be relaxed to require at least one valid representative membership instead.

## 12. Implementation recommendation

Safe next implementation order:
1. Refactor roster/ranking to be Character-unique while Group navigation remains Membership-based.
2. Add Roger / Rayleigh / Gaban.
3. Add Rocks and historical memberships.
4. Add Whitebeard / Kaido / Big Mom as Characters with representative current/own-crew memberships plus historical Rocks membership.
5. Run top-tier recalibration review before merging the new scores into main ranking.
6. Add current Rayleigh as a second Evaluation later if desired; do not duplicate Character identity.
